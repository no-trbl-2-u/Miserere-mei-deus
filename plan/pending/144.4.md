## Decisions
- **D?sim-answers-the-check-by-field** · 2026-11-03 · `CombatSimPolicy.mercyCheck` (`'spare' | 'finish'`) is how a sim answers a check that fires, separate from `mercyChoice`, the Open Hand's old opening. Every existing witness finishes, so no sim changes its pay; `spare-all` and `finish-all` are run policies that differ in nothing else, the Open Hand off in both. *Why:* a comparison must not be confounded by the old Open Hand path, and 144.6 deletes `mercyChoice` while this field stays.
- **D?sim-compare-is-a-printout** · 2026-11-03 · `npm run sim:run -- --compare` prints spare-all against finish-all at the end of Act 1: XP, level, shillings, checks fired and the table's spread, and says when a run fell short. It judges nothing. *Why:* D57 and D88, a measurement is not a licence to tune.
