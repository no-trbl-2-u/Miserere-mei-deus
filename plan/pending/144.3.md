## Decisions
- **D?mercy-check-is-rolled-by-the-host** · 2026-11-03 · `rollMercyCheck` runs on a state already `finalOutcome: 'victory'`, called by the panel as the fight ends, and the engine's own turn loop draws nothing for it. *Why:* Spare marks a win and is no new ending, so every sim and seeded stream reads as before and pays as Finish until 144.4 calls it.
- **D?spare-grants-without-friendship** · 2026-11-03 · A spared foe is `outcome: 'victory'` with `spared: true`, and the reducer grants the flag and journal entry on that; `friendship` stays for the Open Hand alone. *Why:* 144.6 removes `friendship`, and A5 must outlive it.
- **D?open-hand-exploit-skips-the-check** · 2026-11-03 · An Exploit that kills is marked `mercyCheck: 'none'`. *Why:* the Open Hand and its choice stay unchanged until 144.6, and the player has just answered a mercy prompt.
- **D?dev-force-keeps-the-difficulty-law** · 2026-11-03 · The dev switch forces the roll, not the difficulty, so an elite or a boss still never asks. A fight with `persistOutcome` off never asks. *Why:* no test or tool can show a prompt the game could not.
