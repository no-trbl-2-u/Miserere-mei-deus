## Notes
- **135.10 (SM2k) defaults.** A boss fight is one with no way out (the fight seam's `isBoss`); `bossDamagePct` is the share of its VITAE gone when the run's last such fight ended, to one decimal, null if never met and left out of the average. `--json` now prints `{ runs, table }` instead of the bare runs array. A stuck run counts as not cleared and ends where it stuck in the table. The table also prints with `--post`.
