## Decisions
- **D?quest-step-ids** · 2026-11-01 · A `quest-step` run event names its quest by the questline's id and its step by the step number as text (a quest outside a questline: its name and no step). A completion logs through `offerCompletedQuestExtrasAction`, the one door every non-turn-in completion passes; a dialogue-given step logs no `accept`. *Why:* the report joins it with `chapter1Content()`, which reads the questline data, and a quest's name can change where its id and step cannot.
