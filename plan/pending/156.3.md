## Notes
- **156.3 (TY1c) defaults, taken under the pilot.** Every `fontFamily` under `app/` and `components/` reads `TYPE_ROLES.<job>.fontFamily`; a component keeps its own size and line height, so no layout moved (taking the role's size too would have resized every screen). `theme/__tests__/type-roles-only.test.ts` fails a direct `FONTS.`, `TYPE.`, `getTypeWithFallbacks` or a string `fontFamily` there. The job was read from each style's name: figures go to `count` (Grenze) or, at 20 pt and over, `bigNumber` (Spectral); small labels, eyebrows and captions to `eyebrow` (Marcellus SC); in-panel headings to `sectionHead` (MedievalSharp); buttons, chips, tabs and badges to `button` and `chip`; screen titles and names to `title`; map names to `mapName`; foe names to `foeName`; sentences and hints that were tracked capitals or monospace to `rules` (Rosarivo); card text to `cardRules`; italic voice to `narration` or, for a speaker's line, `spoken` (Eagle Lake); quest, chronicle and log lines to `entry` (Fondamento); the dev screens' monospace to `count`. Tracking of 0.5 and over in a moved style became the role's (0 to 0.3), since none of T's faces is condensed capitals.
- **Width-fit sites keep their face and tracking.** The hazard card, the route strip and the paid line's capitals and serif runs are sized from per-face width estimates (the font-width guard), so they took the role on the face they already had (`chip`, `title`, `cardRules`, `narration`, `count`) and kept their authored tracking. The guard's sites now name a role, not a `FONTS` slot. `no-gothic-figures.test.ts` now requires a figure-named style to use `count` or `bigNumber`.
- **`FONTS` and `TYPE` stay exported** for engine code and tests, so a sibling branch that still adds a direct face fails the lint test with a clear line rather than the typecheck. The vendored Bebas Neue, IM Fell English (both) and JetBrains Mono files leave `assets/fonts/` with their licences; Pirata One stays, as the source `scripts/build-title-assets.mjs` draws the splash wordmark from. Every `@expo-google-fonts` package is used by a role (a test now fails a loaded face no role names).
