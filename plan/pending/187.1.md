## Decisions
- **D?mercy-at-the-blow** · 2026-11-22 · The mercy check fires on the lethal blow, not after the win. On 15% of lethal blows against a normal or elite foe, the foe stands at 1 VITAE and asks spare or finish. Finish is the kill; spare is a win that leaves the foe at 1 VITAE. The check gives neither side a turn or an effect. P2 and bearings' "Win condition" are reworded to match. Supersedes D132's after-the-win timing. *Why:* T's rule (2026-11-22); sparing should feel like holding the blow back, and elites can be spared.
