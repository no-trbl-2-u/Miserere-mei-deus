## Decisions
- **D?scene-slot-hosts** · 2026-11-12 · A node's story plays from its scene slot (`registerNodeScene`, rows of `NODE_SCENES` in `content.ts`, registered inside `registerMapEventContent()`), once a run before the node's own event; no Act 1 node's own kind is `cutscene` but the Lantern Deep's two sealed doors. Every host that ends a cutscene (the cutscene screen, the dev skip, a CLI or sim driver) branches on `isBeforeScene` and continues to the node (`continueToNode`, or a second resolve), never just dismisses it. *Why:* a dismissed before-scene leaves its node owed and its own event unplayed.
