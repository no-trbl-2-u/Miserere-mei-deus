## Notes
- **159.5 (TU1e) defaults, taken under the pilot.** Card names are one line on every face (T, 2026-11-08, overriding the spec's two-line Design). The font steps down in half-point steps from the nominal size (13 hand, 20 large, 10.5 Spoils) to a floor of 10, computed from Fondamento advance widths in `card-name-fit.ts` so web and native agree. The longest Act 1 name ("Stir the Thundersnow") gets 10 pt on the 95 pt hand column and about 7.5 pt on the 71 pt Spoils column; the Spoils floor is 7 because that name cannot hold one line at 10 in an 80 pt tile. `compactFree` drops the leading "+" and keeps a "-". The face's frame, name band and ledger are `HUE.black` (`CARD_FACE_BLACK`), and a 12 pt gradient at the art's top and bottom edge blends into it. The Deck's keyword runs draw `run.word` (capitals).
