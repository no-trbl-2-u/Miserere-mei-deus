## Decisions
- **D?play-log-retention** · 2026-11-01 · A `sim` or `harness` row older than 14 days is deleted nightly (05:17 UTC) by the Worker's `scheduled` handler; a `human` row never is, and `ballot_verdicts` is never read. A run's age is `play_runs.started_at`: an old run goes whole in one `db.batch` (its `run_events`, `fights`, `deck_events` and `puzzle_attempts` rows, then its `play_runs` row), run-less and orphan rows go by `received_at`, capped at 200 runs or 1000 rows per batch and 25 batches per night. The window is the constant `RETENTION_DAYS` in `backend/src/retention.ts`. *Why:* T (2026-11-01) wants bot data for 14 days and his own for good; D1 holds about 110-150 MB of bot rows at steady state, and `docs/reports/sim/history.jsonl` keeps the numbers (spec 135).
