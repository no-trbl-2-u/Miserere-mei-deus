## Decisions
- **D?kit-art-fits-the-box** · 2026-11-06 · Where a place keeps today's layout, the ART is sized to the element, never the element to the art: a nine-slice's scale is chosen so its unstretched end ornaments fall inside the padding the element already has. *Why:* T's call on the fight is that only the art changes, so a piece that does not fit is scaled down rather than given room. The reference build did the opposite and grew four boxes.

## Notes
- **152.3 (UK1c) — what shipped.** The five brass places read their look from the table: the reveal's ENTER COMBAT (`action-button-selected`) and WITHDRAW (`action-button-idle`), the intent badge's ring (`combat-intent-oxblood` for a hit, `combat-intent-brass` for anything else, per T's answer to #4) and its damage pill (`combat-badge` at 0.12), the conviction chip (`combat-badge` at 0.17), the player dock (`nav-dock-dark`) and the player VITAE bar (`combat-player-track` with `combat-player-fill`, or `combat-vitae-fill` below a quarter). The name rail, momentum chip, END and portrait ring keep today's code, untouched. No card changed.
- **Every box is where it was, and that is measured, not asserted.** `CombatBoard.layout-pins.test.tsx` renders the board at 390x844 with the phone's insets and pins each element's flattened box against literals captured off `origin/main` before any art went in. It pins the brass places (intent ring 36pt, conviction chip 7/3 padding, dock 10/8/42 with gap 10 and minHeight 106, VITAE bar 8pt), the plain places (momentum chip's `alignSelf: center`, END's drawn border, the 48pt portrait), the exported dock and rune-column constants by name, the look table itself, and that no card in hand draws a kit source. Reproducing the reference build's own drift (VITAE bar 8 to 12) was confirmed to turn the suite red, so the pins bite.
- **The reference build is a trap as much as a reference.** `5dd8cf6e9` (closed PR #1398) is the treatment T approved, but it also moved the intent disc 36 to 44, the dock's side padding 10 to 18, the VITAE bar 8 to 12 and the dock's VITAE column gap 4 to 2 — exactly what T then asked not to happen. It additionally skins END and the ledger's pending marks, which are not brass places, and puts `nav-count-plate-dark` on the MOMENTUM chip, which T kept plain. Copy its piece choices, not its stylesheet.
- **The dock frame is wider than the dock's padding, on purpose.** `nav-dock-dark`'s end caps sit behind the portrait and END rather than pushing them inward. The alternative was to widen the dock's padding as the reference build did, which moves every cell. Holding the layout won.
- **`nav-dock-dark`'s slices were wrong and are now measured.** They were `[0.12, 0.055, 0.12, 0.055]`, carried over from the reference build. The art's rails each carry a centre ornament reaching 37% of the height at the top and 30% at the bottom, so both ornaments sat in the middle band, which stretches 2.7x vertically to fill the dock — it dragged a smeared brass diamond down across the VITAE readout and the pile counts. Now `[0.38, 0.065, 0.3, 0.065]`: both rails whole, only the plain panel between them stretched. The numbers came from a column and row profile of the art, not from taste. **`nav-dock-vellum` carries the same original slices and is the tab bar's piece (152.2's) — it was converted on the same recipe, so it is very likely to have the same defect. It was left alone here.**
- **The phase marks needed wrapping, and that is the RN-web trap in the flesh.** `LedgerMark` returns a bare `Svg`, which on RN-web is not a positioned box, so the dock's absolutely positioned backdrop can paint over it. Each mark is now wrapped in a `View` of its own 14pt size. The marks are otherwise untouched.
- **The telegraph disc keeps today's colours, deliberately.** A `phases` foe draws one large 56pt disc whose colour names which of five kinds is coming. The kit has two rings, oxblood and brass, which cannot carry five kinds, and T's ruling is that the ring keeps carrying information. So the large disc keeps its colour-coded ring and only the ordinary badge and its pill wear brass, as the reference build also did. The board screenshot's foe is a telegraph foe, so the badge it shows is that unchanged disc.
- **Two comment lines on `main` were reworked to unred a shared gate.** `check-comments` was failing on `main` for `looks.ts:3` and `looks.test.ts:22` (both naming spec numbers), which reds `verify-root` for every branch cut from it. Both lines were reworded here without touching `LOOKS` or `PLACE_PIECES`.
