- **159.1 (TU1a) defaults, taken under the loop.** The Spoils panel prints the title, the offers and the buttons only; `SPOILS_SUB` and `ELITE_REWARD_SUB` are deleted. The elite UPGRADE INSTEAD button keeps its own note (T struck only the TAKE NOTHING and TAKE CARD notes). `metaLine` left the view-model, the pane and its test; `phaseBadge`, `roundLabel` and `turnLabel` stay (the board keys a pending-die reset on `turnLabel`). The seam fog is `SeamFog.tsx`: an `AXM.bg` gradient (clear at the top, opaque at the plate's foot), `pointerEvents="none"`, laid between the pane and the interactive column so the hand and dice sit over it. Its top is the foe box's bottom edge: the scene band (`SCENE_BAND_PCT` 62) and the gap under the foe (`FIGURE_BOTTOM_PCT` 9) are now shared constants the pane reads, so the foe's lowest drawn pixel (the figure is bottom-aligned in its box, for every foe) is above the fog's top by construction.
- **Card-face extra scope (from PR 1458's review).** The name band reserves the free-pill room only when the face shows a pill (`nameBandHeight(large, narrow, hasPill)`); a pill-less 80 x 118 Spoils tile now has 45 pt of art (33 with a pill). `NAME_LINE_H` and `NAME_PAD_V` are gone: `PLATE_NAME` and `PLATE_NAME_PAD_V` feed both the `plateName*` styles and the band height. The glyph-mode test budgets from `nameBandHeight(true, false, true)` (68), not a literal 52.
