## Notes
- **153.3 (UX1c) defaults, taken under the loop.** The tab bar drops its labels (`tabBarShowLabel: false`) on both looks, draws every icon at 30pt, and keeps a per-tab `tabBarAccessibilityLabel`. The vellum dock's nine-slice was wrong against the art (800x74, profiled by dark-pixel counts): the end flourishes sit within 66px of each edge and the rail pairs run to row 19 above and from row 64 below, so the slices are now `[0.27, 0.09, 0.14, 0.09]` at scale 0.8. The art is then 59pt in the 60pt bar, so the middle band stretches about 1.02x instead of 1.67x. Spec's guessed 0.15 columns were wider than the ink needs; 0.09 keeps the tab slots 42pt wide at 360. The bar pads 54pt each side, past the flourish. The tab-bar FIT contract in `tabs.engine.ts` still measures label widths that are no longer drawn; it is left in place, harmless, for a later tidy.
