C4 · Level 1 · System context

Who and what touches the game

One person designs and plays it; an autonomous loop builds it through pull requests; a few hosted services carry builds, play logs and research. No external service owns game rules (docs/external-architecture.md, rule 1).

C4 · Level 2 · Containers

Inside the monorepo

Three npm workspaces (engine, app, worker) plus the loop's Markdown state, the DevLog, the art tree and the agent-facing MCP. The app reads the engine as local source, never a published package, so any engine surface change can break the app.

Mechanics · Resource flow

The dice and Conviction economy

Four dice a threat phase, one per colour. A die only powers a card's PAID line; Conviction is spent only on signatures. Every source and sink below names its constant.

Content · Relic → signature → lane

Relics open lanes and grant signatures

A worn relic adds its stat lines to each fight, grants one signature, and opens its lane to the reward pool. A run starts with none; up to three are worn at once (SLOT_CAPACITY.relic).

Mechanics · Stats, VITAE, levels

One bar, three stats, S3 scaling

VITAE is the only health track. Each effect scales by its family's stat: body for damage to the foe, mind for anything on you, heart for anything on the foe.

Your stats
170 max VITAE = 50 + 12·body + 6·mind + 6·heart
S3 scaling (scaleAmount)

One-shot: base × stat ÷ 5. Repeating: base × (stat + 5) ÷ 10. Floors, minimum 1, no cap; durations never scale. SACRIFICE, TETHER and "the foe loses n" are unscaled.

Experience to each level (experienceToNext)

70 XP for level 2, then +10% per step through level 20, +15% to 40, +20% after. A full Act 1 clear ends at level 20, meeting the four doors at 4, 9, 14 and 18. Level 50 costs 229,326 total, about 63 times Act 1's income. Kills pay level × 20 (normal), 50 (elite), 200 (boss).
