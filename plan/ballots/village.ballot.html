The Village, the Blacksmith and the Shop

Five screens each. The first of each is Today; the other four are mockups. Today's Village and Shop are the app's own screenshot (the Shop is a section of that same page); today's Blacksmith is redrawn from its current code.

Tick one per screen. Ticking another moves the tick; each screen has its own note line for a mix.
Every button on the mockups is the brass plate the main menu and Settings wear. The portraits are stand-ins for spacing, from sorted-entity-library-base: Harbalist as the innkeeper, Evil Merchant as the shopkeeper, Stonehead as the smith, Hermit as the host.
The spoken lines are placeholders for your words. The prices are the engine's.
