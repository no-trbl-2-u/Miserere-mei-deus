Blood Price — rehearsal ballot

Paper rehearsal (D73, D74). Family: Blood Price. Three sub-lanes, one per tab: Payoff, Engine, Misc. Each has 10 slots × 3 candidates (90 cards in all); the target is about 1 survivor per slot, 30 in all.

Tick each card you keep. Ticking two in one slot means "merge". Unticked = CUT. Use a slot's note box for a TWEAK (one number) or a merge rule.
SACRIFICE n: lose n VITAE (never below 1). DEAL: damage. GUARD: block. VULNERABLE: the foe takes more damage. Numbers are base values before stat scaling. For reference, A Plain Blow is FREE DEAL 2 / PAID DEAL 5. Globals: FREE ⏳3 = in play 3 turns, PAID ∞ = rest of combat. The line starts with its trigger (⟳ start of your turn, 🩸 when you SACRIFICE, 📜 when you play a Curse); a PAID line shows only what changes.
Scrap floor: scrapping any card gives 1 Conviction, so no line gives only "gain 1 Conviction".
