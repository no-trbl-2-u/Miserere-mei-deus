The Elementalist — rehearsal ballot

Second family rehearsal, repeating the Blood Price process. Family: the Elementalist, "I bend the elements." Three sub-lanes, one per tab: Payoff, Engine, Misc. Each has 10 slots × 3 candidates (90 cards in all); the target is about 1 survivor per slot, 30 in all. Element tags: 🔥 Fire, ❄ Frost, ⚡ Storm; two tags mark a pair bridge.

Tick each card you keep. Ticking two in one slot means "merge". Unticked = CUT. Use a slot's note box for a TWEAK (one number) or a merge rule.
INFERNO n: both burn. The foe loses n VITAE at the start of its turn; you lose half (rounded down) at the start of yours; plain loss, and it can kill you. Placeholder math: 3 turns per application; the split and duration are not ruled yet. "On the foe only" / "you take" lines burn one side.
CHILL n: the foe's attacks deal n less; it drops by 1 each round; more CHILL adds to it. FROZEN: while CHILL is at least the foe's attack, the foe is FROZEN: the next DEAL it takes is doubled, then FROZEN ends but the CHILL stays (it can re-freeze every turn).
STORM n: the foe loses n VITAE at the start of its turn, then the STORM grows by 1; it lasts all combat; more STORM adds to it. DRAW n: draw n cards (a new shared keyword). BEND: turn one element on the foe into another.
DEAL: damage. GUARD: block. HEAL: restore VITAE. VULNERABLE: the foe takes more damage from DEALs. Numbers are base values before stat scaling. For reference, A Plain Blow is FREE DEAL 2 / PAID DEAL 5; Blood Price's Forfeit Strike is SACRIFICE 2, DEAL 7 / SACRIFICE 4, DEAL 16.
Globals: FREE ⏳3 = in play 3 turns, PAID ∞ = rest of combat; all open on ⟳ start of your turn. A PAID line shows only what changes. Scrap floor: no line gives only "gain 1 Conviction".
