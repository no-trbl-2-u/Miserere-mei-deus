# 183 — The three unearnable lane relics join the elite drop pool

> The Bleeding Signet, the Elementalist's Brooch and the Elementalist's Belt can be won from an elite fight, so all six lane relics can be earned on the Breakwater.

## Why

### Sources

- **T, 2026-11-14 (attended, after the Chapter 1 gap audit, `plan/chapter1-gaps.md`):** asked how the three relics no run can earn should be handled, T answered "Add to the elite drop pool".
- **The audit's finding:** of the six lane relics (Blood Price's three and the Elementalist's three, phases LR1d and LR1e), three have a way in and three have none.

### What exists today

- **The library** (`axiomancer-mechanics/src/Items/relic.library.ts`) holds twelve relics, each with a `relicSource`. The six lane relics: Crimson Circlet (`elite`), Ruby Signet (`quest`), Bleeding Signet (`quest`), Elementalist's Signet (`elite`), Elementalist's Brooch (`quest`), Elementalist's Belt (`quest`).
- **The elite drop** (`eliteRelicPool`, `Combat/combat.rewards.ts`) draws from a fight's own drop list, or, when it lists none, from every `relicSource: 'elite'` relic the player does not carry. No Breakwater fight lists drops.
- **The quest chain** pays two relics: the Ruby Signet at step 1 and the Mirror Brooch at step 3 (`World/breakwater-chapter.questline.ts`, `BREAKWATER_CHAPTER_RELICS`).
- **So three of the six are paid by nothing:** `relic-bleeding-signet`, `relic-elementalist-brooch`, `relic-elementalist-belt`. Roster health has pinned the Bleeding Signet as an orphan since 2026-10-13 (phase RH1).
- The library's header already says a source "does not limit who may pay a relic" (T, 2026-10-28), so a relic in the elite pool may still be a quest's reward later.

### The small version first

The pool is read from one field. Changing that field on three entries is the whole task. One chunk.

## Design

- `relicSource` becomes `'elite'` on `relic-bleeding-signet`, `relic-elementalist-brooch` and `relic-elementalist-belt`. The elite pool for a fight with no drop list grows from four relics to seven.
- The library's header table says so for the three, and the roster-health pin for the Bleeding Signet goes, since the relic is now reachable.
- Nothing else about a relic changes: names, text, stats, signatures, costs, icons.
- The drop chance (`ELITE_RELIC_DROP_CHANCE`) and the rule that a carried relic never drops again are unchanged.

### Decisions made upfront (do not ask)

- The three other relics no run can earn (Carapace Belt, Brimming Locket, Penitent's Brooch) are not lane relics of the six and stay as they are. [T to confirm #1]
- No balance retune rides this: the measured-truth report is regenerated by its own job.

### Out of scope

- A quest that pays any of the three.
- Per-fight drop lists on the Breakwater's elites.

## Acceptance

- **A1** A won elite fight with no drop list can drop each of the Bleeding Signet, the Elementalist's Brooch and the Elementalist's Belt; a test shows all three in `eliteRelicPool` for a player who carries none, and roster health lists none of the six lane relics as an orphan.

## Open questions

- [T to confirm #1] The other three unearnable relics (Carapace Belt, Brimming Locket, Penitent's Brooch). Default: left alone. Or: they join the elite pool too.
