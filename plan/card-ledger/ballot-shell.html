The Family — ballot

Family: the one-line fantasy. Three sub-lanes, one per tab: Payoff, Engine, Misc. Each has 10 slots × 3 candidates (90 cards in all); the target is about 1 survivor per slot, 30 in all.

Tick each card you keep. Ticking two in one slot means "merge". Unticked = CUT. Use a slot's note box for a TWEAK (one number), a merge rule, or a colour change ("make it blue").
Colour is pitched by the card's job: red (body) the hits, blue (mind) the walls, healing and utility, purple (heart) the afflictions, grey (any) colourless.
DEAL: damage. GUARD: block. HEAL: restore VITAE. VULNERABLE: the foe takes more damage from DEALs. Numbers are base values before stat scaling. For reference, A Plain Blow is FREE DEAL 2 / PAID DEAL 5.
Globals: FREE ⏳3 = in play 3 turns, PAID ∞ = rest of combat; all open on ⟳ start of your turn. A PAID line shows only what changes. Scrap floor: no line gives only "gain 1 Conviction".
