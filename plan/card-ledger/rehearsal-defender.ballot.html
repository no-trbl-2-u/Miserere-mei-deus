The Defender — ballot

Family: "I'm a turtle with spikes who discards random cards from my own hand for bigger payouts." Three sub-lanes, one per tab: Payoff, Engine, Misc. Each has 10 slots × 3 candidates (90 cards in all); the target is about 1 survivor per slot, 30 in all.

Tick each card you keep. Ticking two in one slot means "merge". Unticked = CUT. Use a slot's note box for a TWEAK (one number), a merge rule, or a colour change ("make it blue").
DEAL: damage. GUARD: block. HEAL: restore VITAE. VULNERABLE: the foe takes more damage from DEALs. DRAW: draw cards.
THORNS N: each foe strike deals N back to the foe (a DEAL); drops by 1 each round. REFLECT N%: each foe strike sends N% of the whole attack back (a DEAL); placeholder: lasts this turn, stacks by adding.
Discard N cards at random appears only on PAID lines: a PAID line you cannot pay cannot be played, and FREE always can. A Spell's PAID line EXILEs it. Numbers are base values before stat scaling. For reference, A Plain Blow is FREE DEAL 2 / PAID DEAL 5.
Globals: FREE ⏳3 = in play 3 turns, PAID ∞ = rest of combat; all open on ⟳ start of your turn. A PAID line shows only what changes. Scrap floor: no line gives only "gain 1 Conviction".
