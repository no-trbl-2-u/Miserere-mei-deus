Enemy movesets

Six foes play moves that are not their own: the Saint Beneath plays the Death Beholder's deck, and five recast foes copy a benched foe's moves. Three recommendations for each of attack, defend, status and danger, for every foe, laid out by its phases.

Tick the moves you want. Ticking more than one in a group means the foe gets them all; tick none and I'll bring new ones. The tag on each card says when it comes in.
Every move uses a rule the game already has. Numbers are first drafts, written at the level foes are tuned from, so they scale with the fight.
WARD: its block. MEND: it heals. SCOUR: it strips a status off itself. TANGLE n: your next n cards cannot use their FREE line. BARNACLE: a Curse in your hand. BRINE: cuts each GUARD you gain. UNDERTOW: a die sits out your next roll. LEECH: drains you and heals it.
