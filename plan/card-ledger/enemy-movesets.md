# Enemy movesets ledger

The ballot `enemy-movesets.ballot.html` (id `enemy-movesets-2026-11-08`) offers moves for six foes that play moves not their own.

## The ask (T, 2026-11-08)

"instead of reskinning the beholder, let's create a whole new moveset for the saint beneath enemy and then replace the death beholder. This way the beholder can keep it's moveset and we can get something more specific to the saint beneath."

"I also want to make sure every animated enemy has a moveset. Make a new ballot with 3 recommendations for each of attack/defend/status/danger for every animated enemy that doesn't have one. Keep in mind the phases"

## Who is on it

| Foe | Tier | Plays today |
|---|---|---|
| The Saint Beneath | boss | a copy of the Death Beholder's deck |
| The Wreckmaster | elite | the Toll-Keeper's moves |
| Saltwick Swarm | normal (elite after the vote) | the Green Jelly's moves |
| Quay-Crab | normal | the Crane-Hook's moves |
| Coralback | normal | the Lamprey-Knot's moves |
| Bilge-Stalker | normal | the Wreck-Picker's moves |

The other 17 animated foes have moves of their own.
