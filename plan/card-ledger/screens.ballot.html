Seven Breakwater screens: ten looks each

Travel, cutscene, quest, loot cache, fight reveal, combat board, and the ends of a fight and a run. In each row A is today's screen and B to J are nine directions.

Tick one look per screen. To mix two looks, tick the nearer one and say what to borrow in the note; a mix gets a second round. BOLD marks the two looks in each row that depart furthest from today.
Every picture is a phone screen at 390 x 844. A wide picture holds two screens of the same look (two cases, or two moments). "Full size" opens the render on its own.
Dotted gold underline: a line invented for the mockup, not final copy. Dashed gold box: an image that does not exist yet, named in the box. Every figure that is not the game's own art is a stand-in. The ledger (screens.md) lists all three.
