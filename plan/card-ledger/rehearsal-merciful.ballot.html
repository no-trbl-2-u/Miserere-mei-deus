The Merciful — ballot

Family: "My mending is a weapon." Three sub-lanes, one per tab: Payoff, Engine, Misc. Each has 10 slots × 3 candidates (90 cards in all); the target is about 1 survivor per slot, 30 in all.

Tick each card you keep. Ticking two in one slot means "merge". Unticked = CUT. Use a slot's note box for a TWEAK (one number), a merge rule, or a colour change ("make it blue").
DEAL: damage. GUARD: block. HEAL: restore VITAE. VULNERABLE: the foe takes more damage from DEALs. DRAW: draw cards.
RADIANCE N: HEAL N, then DEAL what that heal actually restored (0 at full VITAE).
MERCY N: HEAL the foe N, and add N to its MERCY. A 3-turn clock starts with the first MERCY; when it runs out, at the start of the foe's turn, the foe takes twice its MERCY. A new MERCY adds to the total and does not reset the clock.
Placeholders (Open, for the sims): the MERCY payout is plain VITAE loss; the total resets once it pays; a heal on the foe stops at its max VITAE and anything past the max bursts 1:1 as a DEAL; a clock ticked to 0 pays at the start of the foe's next turn. SACRIFICE and LEECH are Blood Price's. Spells (two per sub-lane): FREE is the card as-is; PAID is a huge impact and ends with EXILE. Numbers are base values before stat scaling. For reference, A Plain Blow is FREE DEAL 2 / PAID DEAL 5.
Globals: FREE ⏳3 = in play 3 turns, PAID ∞ = rest of combat; all open on ⟳ start of your turn. A PAID line shows only what changes. Scrap floor: no line gives only "gain 1 Conviction".
