## The map rules this sits on

- Players can backtrack.
- Players can hold more than one quest at a time.
- A quest node blocks passage: the player cannot move past it until its quest is complete.

## The story route

1. **Start, the windmill (W1).** The player is given a quest: find the fishermen whose boat capsized.
2. **The wreck hazard (H4, blue).** Completing it opens the path to the sea cave.
3. **The sea cave (H6)** is a travel node down to the cove below.
4. **The cove elite (C1, green)** blocks the quest node behind it. Killing it reaches **the quest node (C2, red)**: the
   fishermen quest completes and a new quest is given (flavour not decided yet).
5. The new quest leads to **the lighthouse (L5, red)** at the top of the map, also blocked by **an elite (L4,
   green)**. Reaching the lighthouse completes the quest and gives the player **a key**.
6. The key is needed for **the keep's quest node (K1, red)**, just before **the chapter boss (K2, pink)**. Completing
   that quest opens the boss.
7. With the chapter boss dead, **the bridge quest (B1, red, far right)** completes; the bridge was impassable while
   the boss lived. The very next node, **B2**, is the travel node out of the chapter.

## The yellow nodes

Every other node is a normal combat encounter most of the time ("like 80% non-elite"), a hazard, or, occasionally at
a dead end, a cutscene.

## The design intent behind the placements

- **Elites guard quest nodes.** An elite sits on the approach to an important node and must be beaten to reach it
  (the cove, the lighthouse).
- **Cutscenes mark important or dead-end places.** They are flavour for now; later they get folded in as part of
  other nodes rather than standing alone ("that's another time").
- **Gates are story beats.** The wreck hazard opens the sea, the key opens the keep, the boss opens the bridge.

## T's rulings on the yellow nodes (2026-10-28, same session)

- **About half the yellow nodes are combat**, not most of them (the first board had 24 combat nodes; T chose half).
- **Rests:** some yellow nodes become rests, placed close to elites.
- **Loot caches:** a few, leaning on dead ends.
- **Reaching a quest node early** (before holding its quest) does nothing yet: the player is turned away, with story
  flavour written for the refusal.
- The dashed road T5-T6 on the sketch is a plain road.
- **Node kinds stand out:** each kind gets its own coloured, thematic icon instead of a plain circle, the set kept
  close in style, each on a clearing so the map's busy lines do not run through it (T, later the same day).
