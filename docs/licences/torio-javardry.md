## The grant, verbatim

> Javardry用のモンスター画像（フリー素材）を作ってます。
>
> 素材は完全フリーで、商用でも何でも利用するのは自由です。ただし何かあったときの責任は、あなた持ちで頼みます。
>
> 素材を利用する際の報告は不要です。
>
> あと、これらの素材については加工もコラも再配布もAIに食わせるのも自由です。

Translation:

> I make monster images (free materials) for Javardry.
>
> The materials are completely free; you are free to use them commercially or in
> any way. However, responsibility if anything happens rests with you.
>
> No notification is required when you use the materials.
>
> Also, for these materials, modification, collage, redistribution and feeding
> them to AI are all free.

So: commercial use, modification, redistribution and derivative work are all
permitted, with no attribution requirement and no notification. Liability rests
with the user. We credit him anyway, because not being required to is not a
reason not to.

## The carve-out — READ BEFORE COMMERCIAL USE

> なお素材の中にはウィザードリィのオリジナルまんまなモンスターがいくつかあるので注意が必要です。
> 以下に名前を列挙しておくので商用には使わないほうが無難でしょう。

> *Note that among the materials there are several monsters taken straight from
> the original Wizardry, so caution is needed. I list the names below; it would

| name | note |
|---|---|
| Arch Demon | avoid |

| Greater Demon (Reptile version) | avoid — **skull version is OK** |
| Sidelle | avoid |
| Murphy's Ghost | avoid |
| Raver Lord | avoid |
| TDN | avoid |
| Orc (red) | avoid — **other colours OK** |
| Kobold (yellow) | avoid — **other colours OK** |
| Flack (green) | avoid — **other colours OK** |

These are his words about *his* pack, not legal advice, and the risk they
describe is Wizardry's rights holders rather than his. Anything matching this
list must be marked `commercial_use: no` in the catalogue rather than quietly
shipped. The carve-out names only monsters; it reaches none of the dungeon art.

## What this covers

This grant covers the packs the owner downloaded from that Drive folder, and
nothing else. Those packs are **not in this repository** — the trove that held
them is untracked by design. What the repository carries is the art copied out
of them into the game tree, whose `provenance.json` files cite this document.
It does **not** cover `Potential Assets/extract/` (Buriedbornes 2's own
extracted assets), `Potential Assets/MCP-Axiomancer/`, or any other trove
directory. `Potential Assets/icons-TBR/` carries its own separate CC-BY-3.0 /
CC0 terms in that folder's `license.txt`.
