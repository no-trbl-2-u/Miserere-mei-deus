# 2026-11-25 — Chapter 1 now ends, and mercy comes at the blow

> Since the last report Chapter 1 gained an ending: crossing the far bank opens
> a chapter complete screen with the run's numbers. A foe at the edge of death
> now stops at one point of vitae and asks whether you will spare it, and a
> spared elite can pay a relic nothing else pays. Early fights end sooner, the
> Saint Beneath speaks at each phase, and rewards now lean on rarity.

## [ui] The far bank opens a chapter complete screen

**What:** crossing to the far bank now ends Chapter 1 on a chapter complete
screen that reads the run's stats back to you. The run's log is written when
you press CONTINUE, not when you answer the crossing's question (182.1, 182.2).
The loot cache's card view was also re-drawn slightly.

**Why:** until now the chapter simply stopped with no sense of arrival. The
spec's aim was a proper ending for the first chapter; the commits do not record
why the log moved to CONTINUE, only that it did.

**Shot:** chapter-complete — the new chapter complete screen
**Shot:** cache-card — the loot cache's card view, before and after
**Commits:** `d421e618a`, `d1f77617a`

## [mechanics] Mercy is asked at the killing blow

**What:** when a blow would kill an ordinary foe or an elite, the foe stops at
1 vitae and the game asks whether to spare it. A spared elite pays a "Spare"
ending in place of the usual elite relic and lane piece, drawn from its own
table (a relic at 15, shillings at 20) and a relic pool no quest pays (187.1,
187.2).

**Why:** the mercy choice used to come after the fight was already decided.
Asking at the blow makes sparing a real choice made with the foe in front of
you, and the separate relic pool gives mercy a reward that fighting cannot
give. Decisions D196 records the rule.

**No capture:** the change is in when a prompt appears and what a reward is,
and no baseline screen shows it.
**Commits:** `d2812b356`, `005045df1`, `caa931ce3`

## [balance] The opening fights end sooner

**What:** foes at player levels 1 to 3 now carry 1.5 times the vitae, not 2
times; past level 3 the factor stays at 2 (186.1).

**Why:** the spec was titled "the opening fights end sooner": the first fights
ran long for a new pilgrim. The measurement behind the 1.5 figure was not
recorded in the commits.

**No capture:** a number change, with nothing on screen to compare.
**Commits:** `89912f98e`, `e5d4a30de`

## [content] Rewards weigh rarity, elites drop lane relics, the Saint Beneath speaks

**What:** reward rolls now weigh rarity 60/30/10 and an elite offers rares only
(188.2); every card is ranked by its own text (188.1). The Bleeding Signet,
Elementalist's Brooch and Belt now drop from elites (183.1). The Saint Beneath
speaks a line as it enters each phase (177.19). Chapter 1's map blurbs and
mercy lines were set from the ballot picks.

**Why:** the three lane relics could not be earned before, so a player on
those lanes could never reach them; elites now carry them. Ranking cards by
text gives the rarity weights something fixed to stand on (D197, D198). The
reason for the phase lines was not recorded beyond the narrator spec.

**No capture:** these are drops, weights and spoken lines; none changes a
baseline screen.
**Commits:** `90b856829`, `16f09bc7b`, `189536e49`, `d3f7d1729`, `0eaa94df5`
