## [ui] The fight reveal: the foe shown whole, and a title card for the boss

**What:** the screen you see before a fight was redrawn. A normal foe shows
its name, level and health, its figure cut out on a ground that fades away,
then ENTER COMBAT and WITHDRAW. An elite carries an ELITE chip; a normal foe
carries none. A boss gets a held title card instead: two gold rules, its
name huge in gold, its place, and its figure rising from below with "tap to
face it" (FR1a, FR1b).

**Why:** T picked these looks from the screens ballot (normal and elite foes
take look G, the boss takes look F) and asked that no ground end on a
clear hard line at the bottom. The old reveal showed less of the foe than
the fight that followed it.

**Shot:** combat-encounter — the reveal for an ordinary foe, before and after
**Shot:** combat-elite — the reveal with the ELITE chip
**Shot:** combat-boss — the boss's held title card
**Commits:** `a064a61e0`, `dc73be67b`, `573da2c84`

## [ui] The defeat screen says what killed you

**What:** the defeat panel now names its cause, and both the defeat panel and
the Game Over screen were redrawn in the same look as the rest of the
interface (176.4).

**Why:** the screens ballot called for a death that explains itself rather
than a bare "you died"; the spec's reason is that a death you can read is one
you can learn from.

**Shot:** defeat-panel — the new defeat panel, naming its cause
**Shot:** game-over — the Game Over screen in the house look
**Commits:** `336360070`, `152ac6e5f`

## [ui] The upgrade screen at a rest site opens on its figure

**What:** upgrading a card at a rest site, the Anvil or an elite reward now
opens on a figure with a string of beads to turn through your cards, with a
way back out. The pair of before-and-after faces names its side, far beads
cannot be touched by accident, and the elite reward's sheet scrolls so HOLD
stays reachable. The rest title, purse box and the fight showing through
behind it are gone (127.9, 127.10).

**Why:** the elite-reward sheet could push its HOLD button out of reach, which
is a flow that could not be finished from a small phone; the rest of the
change makes the upgrade screen match the other redrawn screens. The wider
reasoning was recorded in the upgrade spec, not in the commits.

**Shot:** rest-upgrade — the new upgrade screen
**Commits:** `ef6c8f247`, `8cfa36cd3`, `c5b4ebb07`, `6b117677e`

lets each be read and bought on its own. No further reason was recorded.
