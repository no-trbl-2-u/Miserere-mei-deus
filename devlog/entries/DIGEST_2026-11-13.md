## [ui] The quest card, the loot cache and the travel screen are redrawn

**What:** the quest screen is now a card with beads and brass buttons, in
several states: the offer, the locked card, the card after you are paid, and a
giver who speaks two lines. The card column scrolls when it is too tall, with a
cue at its foot while the buttons lie below the fold, and the relic banner sits
under the card. The loot cache gained its goods and card views, and the travel
screen was redrawn (173.2, 173.3, 177.16).

**Why:** the card is built to the screens ballot's look, as the other redrawn
screens were. The scrolling cue answers a flow that could not be finished: on
a small phone the buttons of a tall quest card sat out of reach. The wider
reasoning for the cache and travel looks was recorded in their specs, not in
the commits.

## [ui] Cutscenes turn their pages at the end of a sentence

**What:** when a cutscene panel runs too long for the screen it is split into
pages that break at a sentence end, never mid-sentence (177.15).

**Why:** a long panel could run past the bottom of the screen. Paging at
sentences keeps every line readable on a small phone. No further reason was
recorded.

## [mechanics] Withdraw and flee send you back to where you came from

**What:** leaving a fight by withdrawing or fleeing now returns you to the
previous node, and the second line on the WITHDRAW button is gone (181.1).

**Why:** the old behaviour left the player on the node whose fight they had
just refused, so the fight could be re-entered at once. The spec records
that a retreat should cost distance, though the commits do not say more.

**No capture:** the change is in where you stand afterwards, and no baseline
screen shows it.
**Commits:** `f34b7d6ee`
