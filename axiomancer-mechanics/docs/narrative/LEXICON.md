# Narrative Lexicon

This supplements the mechanics [canonical lexicon](../LEXICON.md); it never
overrides it. Terms below are authoring tools, not new engine keywords.

## Core world vocabulary

| Term | Use | Do not overextend into |
|---|---|---|
| claim | a rule, belief, entitlement, or assertion someone acts upon | synonym for every thought |
| seam | a place where claims or systems meet and create friction | generic portal or magical glow |
| proofmark | a physical sign that a test or inspection occurred | automatic proof that it was honest |
| countermark | a later correction, refusal, or rival authority's mark | generic decoration |
| keeping | custody with obligation; preserving a thing for someone | generic inventory storage |
| forfeit | a known thing surrendered by rule or choice | surprise punishment |
| residue | persistent material or social result of an event | vague sadness |
| allowance | tolerated margin in craft, law, or agreement | combat resource unless implemented |
| witness | person or object that can support an account | omniscient truth guarantee |
| join | fitted meeting of materials or plans | universal metaphor in adjacent sentences |
| tally | count with ownership and purpose | atmospheric runes |
| revision | an explicit changed claim or practice | consequence-free reset |

## Place-specific word banks

Select exact words that belong to the place and action.

- **Fishing Village:** caulking, warp, cleat, sounding line, fish scale, tide
  ledger, net needle, smoke rack, ferry pin, salt bloom.
- **Northern Forest:** coppice, cambium, resin pocket, root plate, windthrow,
  blaze mark, fungal shelf, leaf mould, charcoal clamp, spring run.
- **Labyrinth/Central City:** course, hinge cup, survey nick, queue token,
  aperture, patched vault, permit seal, worn threshold, service passage.

Use common language around specialist nouns so comprehension does not depend on
trade knowledge.

## Philosophical vocabulary

Use premise, inference, contradiction, exception, category, consequence,
evidence, cause, and witness when a person is actually reasoning. Characters
need not know academic labels to enact a fallacy. The event should demonstrate
the error before the codex names it.

Capitalization is reserved for implemented systems, statuses, card names,
proper institutions, and UI labels. Do not capitalize an abstraction merely to
make it sound ancient.

## Mechanics-safe vocabulary

- **VITAE:** canonical combat vitality label. Keep exact in UI and mechanical
  previews.
- **Colour:** a card's or a die's heart / body / mind identity. Say
  "colour", never "stance" (the stance layer was removed, D65).
- **Status effects:** use the implemented status name and exact stack/duration
  syntax in rules copy. Narrative aliases may appear only in a separate flavor
  line.
- **Heart / Body / Mind:** retain capitalization when referring to implemented
  stats, dice, or stances; use lowercase for ordinary anatomy or thought.

Before shipping a new capitalized term, confirm it exists in the engine or is a
proper noun. Do not create narrative synonyms for mechanical concepts.

## Diction boundaries

Prefer: fitted, patched, counted, carried, scraped, swollen, warped, revised,
withheld, apportioned, recorded, returned.

Use sparingly: ancient, cursed, sacred, endless, forgotten, void, destiny,
madness. Each requires scene evidence and should not carry atmosphere by itself.

Ban from house narration:

- faux-archaic constructions (`thee`, `thou`, `hath`, ornamental inversions);
- generic “wrathful heavens,” “blood-red sky,” judgmental thunder, or other
  scriptural-weather shorthand;
- prestige-dark filler such as ineffable evil, nameless dread, or a land that
  “remembers” without a concrete recording mechanism;
- imported catchphrases or recognizable strings from external fiction.

## Naming method

Name places and objects from function plus local revision: **Split-Pin Ford**,
**Third-Cut Coppice**, **Recaulked Gate**. Name institutions from what they claim
to administer: **Office of Measures**, **Keepers of the North Passage**. Check
that names are pronounceable, distinct in UI, and not all built from the same
compound pattern.
