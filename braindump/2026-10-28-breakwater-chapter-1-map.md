The shipped Breakwater (22 nodes, a four-step questline on fights and hazards, QL1d) is not the chapter T sees. T's

## Prior art consulted
(memory read, not a KB citation)
- **Slay the Spire — one icon per room type on the act map:** monster, elite, rest, merchant, treasure, unknown each
  have a distinct glyph, so routes are planned at a glance; widely praised as the genre's readability baseline.
- **Hades — door reward previews:** the next chamber's reward shows as a symbol on the door; players cite it as what
  makes route choice feel informed.
- **The Legend of Zelda dungeons / Hollow Knight — lock-and-key gating:** a visible locked way plus a key found
  elsewhere turns backtracking into a goal; well received when the lock is shown before the key.
- **Darkest Dungeon — room and corridor icons revealed by scouting:** kind shown before entry; reception mixed on
  hidden information, positive on the icons themselves.

## Directions considered
### Option A — Rebuild in place on the same Breakwater id, nodes renamed to the board's codes
The engine map keeps its name; node ids become `bw-w1`..`bw-p3`, the board's codes, so T and the loop say the same
thing. **Trade-off:** a save inside the old Breakwater must be moved (its node ids vanish). **Telltale failure
mode:** a mid-chapter save loads onto a node that no longer exists.
### Option B — Keep `bw-1..bw-22` and append `bw-23..bw-43`
Least save churn. **Trade-off:** ids say nothing; every talk about a node needs the lookup table forever.
**Telltale failure mode:** T says "H4" and a brief says "bw-11".
Chosen: A, with a save step that restarts a save inside the Breakwater at W1, character kept [T to confirm].

### Icons: two treatments on the board
Token (coloured disc, light or dark glyph) and silhouette (coloured glyph on a dark pool). Board default: token.
**Trade-off:** tokens read best at phone size; silhouettes show more of the plate. **Telltale failure mode:** two
kinds blur together at the 0.6 zoom floor (quest scroll vs cutscene quill is the pair to watch).
